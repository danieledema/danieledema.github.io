### Summary
This work uses 3D foundation models to estimate relative poses between images captured by different robots, even when viewpoints differ substantially. Outlier rejection and pose-graph optimisation address unreliable matches and scale ambiguity, enabling more accurate and efficient decentralised collaborative mapping.
