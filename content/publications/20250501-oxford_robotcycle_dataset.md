### Summary
The Oxford RobotCycle dataset combines observations from a cyclist-mounted sensor platform with eye tracking and traffic analysis. It supports research into road-user interactions, cycling risk, scene understanding and motion estimation, providing evidence for safer urban cycling infrastructure.
