Spotlight presentation at ECCV 2026.

### Summary
PriorEye augments an end-to-end driving policy with street-level images associated with the intended route. Contextual and fallback memories supply advance visual information and regulate its influence, improving planning while handling corrupted sensors or unreliable retrieved priors.
