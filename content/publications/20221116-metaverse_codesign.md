### Summary
This work coordinates sensing frequency, data transmission and prediction to keep a physical device and its digital counterpart synchronised while limiting communication. A constrained reinforcement-learning policy balances tracking accuracy and bandwidth, with validation on a robotic-arm digital-twin prototype.
