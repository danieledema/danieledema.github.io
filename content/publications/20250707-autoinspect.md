### Summary
AutoInspect integrates mapping, localisation, graph-based navigation, scheduling and mission execution into a modular autonomy system for inspection and monitoring. Long deployments on a Spot robot at a robotics test site and a fusion facility demonstrate sustained autonomous operation with configurable sensing and computing hardware.
